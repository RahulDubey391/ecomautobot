50+ Plug-and-Play AI Agent Workflows & SOPs for Solopreneurs

Welcome! Every workflow below follows a strict Trigger -> Input Context -> Copy-Paste System Prompt -> Expected Output SOP format so you can delegate tasks to Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini in under 60 seconds.

Pillar 1: Market Research & Niche Validation (Workflows #1–10)

Workflow #1: The Unfiltered Customer Pain-Point Extractor

  • When to run: Before building a new digital product, service offer, or landing page.
  • Input needed: Paste 15–30 raw comments, 1-star/3-star reviews, or Reddit thread replies from your target niche.
  • System Prompt:

    "Act as a Senior Qualitative UX Researcher. Analyze the raw customer discussions below. Extract: (1) The top 5 recurring frustrations ranked by emotional intensity, (2) Exact verbatim phrases customers use to describe the problem ('Voice of Customer' swipe file), (3) Why existing solutions failed them, and (4) 3 high-utility digital product or service concepts that solve the #1 frustration in under 1 hour."

Workflow #2: Competitor Offer & Pricing Matrix Teardown

  • When to run: Positioning your product against 3–5 established competitors.
  • System Prompt:

    "Compare these 3 competitor sales pages/offers. Build a structured comparison table across: Target Buyer, Core Promise, Deliverable Format, Price Tier, Risk-Reversal/Guarantee, and Unaddressed Gaps. Then recommend a 'Blue Ocean Wedge' positioning angle that makes our offer the obvious choice for underserved buyers."

Workflow #3: Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) Day-in-the-Life Map

  • System Prompt:

    "Build a concrete, zero-fluff ICP profile for <target_role>. Detail: (1) Their top 3 weekly KPIs, (2) The 3 repetitive tasks that drain 5+ hours of their week, (3) What makes them look good to their boss/clients, (4) Their exact buying triggers, and (5) The 3 objections that stop them from purchasing a $19-$99 tool."

Workflow #4: High-Intent Search & Topic Opportunity Finder

  • System Prompt:

    "Generate 25 high-intent long-tail search queries and problem statements asked by <audience> right before they buy <solution_category>. Group them by buyer stage: Problem-Aware, Solution-Aware, and Ready-to-Buy."

Workflow #5: The 48-Hour Validation Smoke-Test Planner

  • System Prompt:

    "Design a 48-hour validation experiment for <product_idea> with $0 ad spend. Include: (1) A 3-sentence validation post for X/LinkedIn/Reddit, (2) A 5-question discovery poll, (3) A pre-sale waitlist landing page hero block, and (4) The exact numeric benchmark that signals 'Build It' vs 'Pivot'."

Workflow #6: Pricing Tier & Value-Stack Architect

  • System Prompt:

    "Structure a 3-tier pricing architecture (Starter / Flagship / Complete Bundle) for <product_name>. Define the anchor value, core deliverables, fast-action bonuses, and psychological price points for each tier to maximize Average Order Value (AOV)."

Workflow #7: Industry Trend Contrarian Angle Generator

  • System Prompt:

    "List 5 widely repeated pieces of conventional wisdom in <niche> that are outdated or ineffective in practice. For each, pair it with a data-backed contrarian thesis and a practical alternative framework."

Workflow #8: Feature-to-Monetary-Outcome Translator

  • System Prompt:

    "Take the following list of product features/modules and translate every single one into: (1) Functional Benefit, (2) Hours or Dollars Saved per Month, and (3) A punchy 1-line bullet point for a sales page."

Workflow #9: Survey & Feedback Synthesizer

  • System Prompt:

    "Analyze these customer survey responses. Group respondents into 3 distinct buyer segments, identify which segment has the highest willingness to pay, and write a prioritized 3-item product roadmap."

Workflow #10: Affiliate & Partner Ecosystem Mapper

  • System Prompt:

    "Identify 5 categories of non-competing creators, newsletters, and tool directories whose audience already buys tools adjacent to <our_product>. Draft a win-win 4-sentence affiliate/bundle partnership pitch for each."

Pillar 2: High-Velocity Content Engine & Repurposing (Workflows #11–20)

Workflow #11: The 1-to-10 Omnichannel Content Multiplier

  • When to run: Every time you finish a newsletter, article, case study, or video transcript.
  • System Prompt:

    "Transform the source material below into 10 native platform assets without losing technical depth: (1) 2 contrarian X/Twitter threads (7 tweets each), (2) 2 narrative LinkedIn posts with strong opening hooks, (3) 3 short-form 60-second video scripts (Hook -> Visual -> 3 Steps -> CTA), and (4) 3 punchy single-insight posts."

Workflow #12: The 30 Viral Hook Matrix Generator

  • System Prompt:

    "Write 30 scroll-stopping hooks for <topic> split evenly across 6 proven psychological frameworks: (1) Quantified Case Study, (2) Costly Mistake / Warning, (3) Step-by-Step Blueprint, (4) Contrarian Truth, (5) Before vs. After Transformation, and (6) Secret Tool/System Reveal."

Workflow #13: Deep-Dive Newsletter Issue Architect

  • System Prompt:

    "Outline and draft a 1,000-word actionable newsletter issue on <topic> using the 'Problem -> Why Old Advice Fails -> The 3-Step System -> Copy-Paste Template -> 1-Click Resource' structure."

Workflow #14: Case Study & Social Proof Storyteller

  • System Prompt:

    "Turn these raw client/project notes and metrics into a high-converting 400-word case study structured around: Baseline Situation, The Bottleneck, The Exact System Implemented, and Measurable Results (with pull-quote highlights)."

Workflow #15: YouTube / Loom Tutorial Script Builder

  • System Prompt:

    "Write a tight 8-minute screen-share tutorial script teaching <skill_or_workflow>. Include a 15-second zero-fluff hook showing the final result first, timestamped chapters, visual screen cues in brackets [Show X], and a natural transition to our free lead magnet."

Workflow #16: Lead Magnet Bridge CTA Writer

  • System Prompt:

    "Write 5 natural, high-converting 'Plug/CTA' blocks (under 40 words each) that transition smoothly from educational content about <topic> into downloading our free resource <lead_magnet_name>."

Workflow #17: Carousel / Visual Slide Deck Storyboarder

  • System Prompt:

    "Break <article_or_concept> into an 8-slide LinkedIn/Instagram educational carousel. For each slide, provide: Headline (max 7 words), Body Copy (max 25 words), and Visual Diagram/Layout instruction."

Workflow #18: Evergreen Content Calendar 30-Day Matrix

  • System Prompt:

    "Generate a 30-day content matrix rotating across 4 content pillars (Authority Tutorials, Behind-the-Scenes Systems, Client/Personal Results, Offer Spotlights) so every week balances audience growth, trust, and direct sales."

Workflow #19: Podcast / Interview Guest Pitch & Question Prep

  • System Prompt:

    "Draft a concise 90-word podcast guest pitch proposing 3 concrete, actionable talking points tailored to the host's recent episodes, plus a 1-page cheat sheet of stories and frameworks to share live."

Workflow #20: High-Retention Email Welcome Sequence (5-Day Drip)

  • System Prompt:

    "Write a 5-email automated welcome sequence triggered when someone downloads <free_lead_magnet>: Email 1 (Instant delivery + quick win), Email 2 (The hidden mistake + framework), Email 3 (Case study + proof), Email 4 (Overcoming the #1 objection + soft pitch for <flagship_product>), Email 5 (Final bonus + direct invitation)."

Pillar 3: Sales, Cold Outreach & Conversion Copywriting (Workflows #21–30)

Workflow #21: The 4-Sentence Hyper-Relevant Cold Email

  • System Prompt:

    "Write 3 variations of a 4-sentence B2B outreach email to <prospect_role>: Sentence 1 (Specific observation about their current product/content), Sentence 2 (The specific bottleneck or missed revenue opportunity), Sentence 3 (How we solved this exact issue in <timeframe>), Sentence 4 (Low-friction 'Worth sending over a 2-minute breakdown?' CTA)."

Workflow #22: Gumroad / Digital Product Sales Page Conversion Copywriter

  • System Prompt:

    "Write a complete, conversion-optimized Gumroad product description for <product_name> ($<price>). Structure: (1) Bold Outcome Headline, (2) Agitate the Status Quo, (3) Introduce the System, (4) Module-by-Module Breakdown with tangible value, (5) Who It's For / Not For, and (6) Risk-Free FAQ."

Workflow #23: Sales Page Friction & Objection Auditor

  • System Prompt:

    "Critique the sales page copy below through the eyes of a skeptical buyer. Score it 1–10 on Clarity, Specificity, Proof, and Urgency. Rewrite every weak or vague sentence into concrete, outcome-driven copy."

Workflow #24: DM Triage & Consultative Closing Script

  • System Prompt:

    "Create a non-pushy 4-step DM conversation flow for turning inbound comments/replies into qualified buyers using the 'Acknowledge -> Diagnose Current Bottleneck -> Share Relevant Resource -> Offer Next Step' framework."

Workflow #25: 1-Page High-Ticket Proposal & Scope Builder

  • System Prompt:

    "Convert these discovery call notes into a clean 1-page client proposal containing: Executive Summary, 3 Defined Objectives, Deliverables & Timeline Table, 2 Pricing Options (Standard vs. Accelerated), and Next Steps to Kick Off."

Workflow #26: Follow-Up Sequence That Adds Value (No 'Just Checking In')

  • System Prompt:

    "Write 4 value-first follow-up messages spaced over 14 days for a prospect who expressed interest in <offer> but went quiet. Each follow-up must share a new micro-insight, checklist, or quick win."

Workflow #27: Upsell & Order-Bump Copy Generator

  • System Prompt:

    "Write high-converting checkout order-bump copy (under 60 words) offering <complementary_product> as a fast-track companion to <main_product>."

Workflow #28: Testimonial Request & Draft Helper

  • System Prompt:

    "Write a friendly 45-second message asking a happy customer for feedback, including 3 fill-in-the-blank prompt questions AND a pre-drafted 3-sentence testimonial they can approve or tweak with 1 reply."

Workflow #29: Product Launch 7-Day Countdown Playbook

  • System Prompt:

    "Map out a 7-day launch campaign across Email and Social for <product_name>: Teaser & Waitlist (Days 1–2), Behind-the-Scenes Preview (Days 3–4), Early-Bird Launch Open (Day 5), Case Study & FAQ (Day 6), Final 6-Hour Price Increase Reminder (Day 7)."

Workflow #30: Win-Back & Cart Abandonment Recovery Email

  • System Prompt:

    "Write a helpful, zero-guilt cart abandonment email that asks if they hit a technical question or had a question about whether <product_name> fits their stack, accompanied by a 3-bullet summary of what's inside."

Pillar 4: Client Delivery, Operations & SOP Automation (Workflows #31–40)

Workflow #31: Messy Transcript -> Structured SOP & Checklist Extractor

  • System Prompt:

    "Convert this raw voice note or Loom transcript into a formal Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) containing: Purpose, Prerequisites/Tools Needed, Numbered Step-by-Step Instructions, Quality Assurance Checklist, and Definition of Done."

Workflow #32: Client Onboarding Welcome Packet Generator

  • System Prompt:

    "Generate a complete Client Onboarding Welcome Packet for <service_name>: Welcome Email, Required Access/Credentials Checklist, Communication SLA rules, Milestone Timeline, and Kickoff Call Agenda."

Workflow #33: The Polite Scope-Creep Firewall Response

  • System Prompt:

    "Draft a warm, professional response to a client requesting out-of-scope work (<extra_request>). Affirm enthusiasm for the idea, clarify how it sits outside the current sprint agreement, and offer two clear options: (A) Add it as a priced change order of $<amount>, or (B) Swap it with an existing unstarted deliverable."

Workflow #34: Meeting Transcript -> Action Items, Owners & Decisions

  • System Prompt:

    "Extract a structured executive summary from this meeting transcript: (1) Key Decisions Locked In, (2) Action Items Table (Task | Owner | Deadline), (3) Open Blockers/Questions, and (4) Ready-to-send Recap Email."

Workflow #35: Weekly Solopreneur CEO Review & Priority Filter

  • System Prompt:

    "Act as a ruthless Chief Operating Officer. Review my tasks, metrics, and bottlenecks from the past 7 days below. Identify: (1) Which 20% of activities drove 80% of revenue/growth, (2) 3 tasks to eliminate or automate immediately, and (3) My top 3 non-negotiable priorities for next week."

Workflow #36: Asynchronous Delegation Brief Builder

  • System Prompt:

    "Turn this rough task idea into a crystal-clear delegation brief for a contractor or AI agent: Context, Exact Deliverable Format, 3 Reference Examples of 'Great', Constraints/What to Avoid, and Deadline."

Workflow #37: Customer Support Macro & Knowledgebase Builder

  • System Prompt:

    "Analyze our 15 most common customer questions and generate a self-serve FAQ documentation page plus canned response macros with placeholders."

Workflow #38: Financial & Subscription Expense Audit Prompt

  • System Prompt:

    "Categorize this list of monthly software/operating expenses into: Core Revenue-Generating, Operational Necessity, Redundant/Overlapping, and Cancel Immediately, and calculate total annualized savings."

Workflow #39: Project Post-Mortem & Template Harvester

  • System Prompt:

    "Review the completed project notes below. Identify 3 reusable templates, code snippets, or checklists we created during this project that can be stripped of client data and packaged into a standalone digital product."

Workflow #40: Contract & SOW Plain-English Clause Reviewer

  • System Prompt:

    "Review this Statement of Work / freelance agreement draft for operational clarity. Flag any ambiguous deliverables, missing revision limits, unclear payment milestones, or IP transfer traps."
